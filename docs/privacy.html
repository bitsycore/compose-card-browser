Privacy Policy

TCG Explorer · last updated 14 September 2026

TCG Explorer does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. The app has no server of its own, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app keeps is stored on your own device and never leaves it:

  • Card data — the sets and cards you browse or download, in a local database.
  • Card images — in a local image cache.
  • Your settings — chosen games, card language, favourite sets, theme and storage limits.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. You can delete any of it at any time from the app's Storage screen, and all of it by uninstalling the app.

Requests the app makes

TCG Explorer has no backend. To show you cards, it requests them directly from each game's public card database, and requests card images from those databases' image servers.

These are independent third parties. Like any website you visit, they necessarily receive your IP address, the request itself (for example, which set you opened) and the app's user agent string, which is TCGExplorer/1.0. They do not receive your name, your device's identifiers, or anything else about you, because the app does not have or send any of it.

Links out of the app

Some screens offer links to Cardmarket, TCGplayer, a card source's own website, or the project's Discord. These open in your browser and only when you tap them. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising, no social features and no user-generated content.

Your data, and your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing for us to look up, export, correct or delete on your behalf, and no data is ever sold or shared. The data on your device is entirely under your control: the Storage screen removes any part of it, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the previous versions remain in the project's public source history.
